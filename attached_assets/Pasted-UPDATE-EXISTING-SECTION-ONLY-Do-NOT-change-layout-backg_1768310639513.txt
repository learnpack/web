TAB CONTENT:

SALES

Common training challenge  
Sales teams struggle to apply product knowledge in real conversations. Training is often theoretical, outdated, or disconnected from real objections and deal scenarios.

How LearnPack adapts the course  
The same product training course is reframed around sales conversations:
- Real objection-handling scenarios  
- Discovery and qualification frameworks  
- Practice focused on decision-making, not memorization  

Expected outcome  
Reps gain confidence faster, apply knowledge in live calls, and shorten ramp-up time without adding extra training programs.

-------------------------
SUPPORT

Common training challenge  
Support teams receive large amounts of documentation but lack structured guidance on how to troubleshoot issues under pressure.

How LearnPack adapts the course  
LearnPack restructures the course around problem resolution:
- Incident-based learning paths  
- Step-by-step diagnostic thinking  
- Reinforcement of safe-first actions and escalation criteria  

Expected outcome  
Faster issue resolution, fewer escalations, and more consistent support quality across the team.

-------------------------
OPERATIONS

Common training challenge  
Operational teams need consistency. Training often explains processes but fails to show how small deviations create bottlenecks and rework.

How LearnPack adapts the course  
The course is adapted to focus on workflows and execution:
- Visual process breakdowns  
- Identification of failure points  
- Decision-making at handoff moments  

Expected outcome  
More predictable execution, reduced operational errors, and smoother cross-team collaboration.

-------------------------
MANAGER

Common training challenge  
Managers are expected to reinforce training outcomes but are rarely trained on how to evaluate real learning versus completion.

How LearnPack adapts the course  
The course shifts from execution to evaluation:
- Coaching frameworks  
- Skill assessment rubrics  
- Signals that indicate applied learning, not just progress  

Expected outcome  
Managers can coach more effectively, identify gaps early, and ensure training translates into performance.

-------------------------
NEW HIRE

Common training challenge  
New hires are overwhelmed by information and often lack clarity on priorities during their first weeks.

How LearnPack adapts the course  
The onboarding course is structured around guidance and progression:
- Clear first-week paths  
- Guided checklists with milestones  
- Context-aware support when learners get stuck  

Expected outcome  
Faster onboarding, higher confidence, and earlier autonomy without increasing training workload.
