CORE POSITIONING (DO NOT DEVIATE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This page is about the Instructional Designer AI.
NOT the AI Tutor.

The Instructional Designer is the AI that helps teams GENERATE complete courses from source material.
The core idea is:
- Course regeneration is faster and cheaper than course maintenance.
- When content changes, you regenerate a new course.
- Courses are disposable and versioned, not patched.

The AI Tutor is SECONDARY and must only appear as a consequence of publishing a course, never as the hero or main value.

Do NOT use language about “maintaining courses”.
Do NOT position ongoing updates as a workflow.
Always frame updates as regeneration from new source material.

Do NOT use em dashes in any text.
