## 1. EL NUEVO ENFOQUE (CLARO Y SIMPLE)

La idea central que debe entender cualquiera que lea la página es esta:

> **Hyperpersonalized Learning in LearnPack is a system, not just an AI tutor.**

La hiperpersonalización ocurre en **tres capas**:

1. **Base course built for the organization**
2. **Learner profiling through smart questions**
3. **Adaptive course experience per individual**

El AI Tutor es una herramienta dentro de ese sistema, no el sistema completo.

Este enfoque:

* Es más B2B
* Explica por qué LearnPack es distinto
* Evita sonar redundante o genérico
* Es muy fácil de entender y explicar a un CEO o Head of L&D

## 3. NUEVA ESTRUCTURA PROPUESTA (SUSTITUYENDO SECCIONES)

### 🔹 HERO (reformulado)

**Headline**
Hyperpersonalized learning, built for organizations and tailored to every learner.

**Subheadline**
LearnPack turns a single training course into a unique learning experience for each employee, adapting content, examples, and explanations to how every person learns.

CTA igual que ahora.

---

### 🔹 SECTION 1 – The Hyperpersonalization System (NUEVA)

**Headline**
How hyperpersonalized learning actually works.

Aquí explicamos el proceso real, sin humo.

#### Step 1. Build the base course for your organization

Organizations create one training course aligned with company goals, tools, language, and internal guidelines.

*(Aquí entra lo que ya hacen hoy con generación de cursos.)*

#### Step 2. Understand each learner as an individual

Before starting, LearnPack asks each learner a small set of questions (around 5) to understand their background, role, experience level, and learning preferences.

Ejemplos de preguntas (breves):

* Role or department
* Previous experience
* Learning style preferences
* Context they relate to

#### Step 3. Adapt the course to each person

LearnPack dynamically adapts examples, explanations, and references so every learner receives the same knowledge, explained in a way that makes sense to them.
